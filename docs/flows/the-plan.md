# The plan: from the first questions to the deadline

> **Label: In force** · one of the rules, read in N95's order. Every doc and its label: [the master doc](https://github.com/wilplus/backend-cursor/blob/main/README.md) (N106).

One page for the diagnosis and the plan: the questions a new speaker answers
in the Lounge before the first Take, what the diagnosis reads, the plan card
and the two calls with the coach, the practice days, changing the plan, and
the plan's end at the deadline. Started 2026-10-26 (backend N160) from the
decisions log's entries N129 to N157, folded here into the rules as they
stand; each later entry about the plan is folded in the same way. The entries
keep their N-numbers and stay the record.

**Built, on for ring 5.** The build started on 26 October 2026, one to one
with the prototype (N203, which lifts N147's "not yet"). The questions, the
calls, the plan card, the practice days in the chat, changing the plan and
the plan's end are built; their migration, with the ring row `plan` at ring 5
(N204), merged on 27 October 2026 (backend #1394). The level's moment in the
walk and a level moving on followed the same day (N209). §2 says what is
built and what is still to come.

**How this page works**

- It works like [`feedback-walk-and-chat.md`](feedback-walk-and-chat.md):
  §1 is the rules as decided, rewritten in place when a rule changes, with the
  old wording as one line in §6. Never a new file.
- Who said it is a suffix in brackets: the backend decisions log's number.
- The prototype is the design (§5). Where it and this page disagree, say so
  in §3.
- No new words on screen without the founder's OK (Never 4): §1 K lists the
  signed words and the ones still waiting.
- It borders three pages: the walk's page owns the walk and its tempo step;
  the recording screens' page owns the set-up and the feelings check; the
  consent screens' page owns the acceptance. This page says when they meet
  the plan.

## 1. The rules

### A · Who gets it

1. Every new speaker is diagnosed and gets a plan. The questions come once,
   after consent and before the first Take, and can't be skipped; only the
   date can (N136, N146).
2. A speaker who already has Takes is asked only "When is your deadline?",
   after their next Take, never before it. Their plan is built from the Takes
   they have (N147, N178).
3. One plan runs at a time. A new event gets the questions again and a new
   plan; the old plan stays in the history (N140). Its questions come after a
   Take, never before one (N178).
4. Before any Take but the first, the only thing shown is the feelings check,
   one tap, with a Skip (D4). Every other question on this page waits until
   after the Take (Never 3; N178).
5. While a speaker is onboarded, the principles wait: from the first
   question until a new speaker's first Take, and while the questions are
   asked for a speaker who already has Takes (A2, A3). The plan says so
   (`onboarding`), and nothing of the voice notebook, the Sunday note or life
   goals shows meanwhile ([principles](principles.md) A6; N243).

### B · The questions, in the Lounge

1. "Agree and continue" on the consent screen opens the Lounge with the
   questions, not the empty Lounge (N146).
2. "When is your deadline?", answered with Pick a date (the calendar) or "No
   date yet". No date gives two months, and Will says so in words: "That's
   fine. Let's give it two months, until 19 December." (N135, N137, N140).
3. "I assume you came here to practise for some important event in your
   life. Please tell me, why does it matter and what is it about?" One voice
   message, recorded with the walk practise's recorder: the strip and the red
   Stop, no Skip. It lands as the speaker's orange voice bubble, and it goes
   through the processing authorization like every recording (N135, N136,
   N137).
4. Will's reply names the speaker's event, date and reason in their own
   words, then says "I can already hear your main vocal cues and verbal
   habits." and "Let me draft your plan. A few more questions first."
   (N138, N150).
5. Up to three follow-ups, one at a time, each answered by voice with the
   same "Record your answer". The key line always comes first: "If {the
   board} remembers one sentence from you, what is it? Say it the way you'll
   say it." Then the first one this speaker hasn't answered yet, in this
   order: audience, talk length and slides, worry, experience, success,
   opening (N139, N140).
6. Each question is a signed template the AI fills with the speaker's own
   words. It may open by naming what they just said, and it leaves out a part
   that can't apply (a toast gets no slides question); a worry they named is
   asked about in their words. The AI's rules: only their words and facts, no
   judgement of how they spoke, no numbers, no promises, nothing about fixing
   them. A question that doesn't match its template, or an AI that fails,
   gives the plain template (N145, N150).
7. The set-up's first question, "What is the topic?", arrives filled in from
   the voice message; the speaker can change it (N141).

### C · The diagnosis

1. It reads the voice: speed, speed on the key line, pauses, filler and
   repeated words, hedges, pitch range, endings, stress on key words, the
   opening's energy, the most confident moment. And the words: the event, why
   it matters, the topic, the key line, the audience, the length, the slides,
   the worry. Fit in the time is measured on every Take, against the length
   set in the set-up (N138, N140).
2. Levels come only from what is measured today. Readings still in their
   shadow stage (filler words, hedges, restarts, a flat or quiet voice) may be
   used; the founder checks each new plan for the first weeks. "A voice that
   moves" uses pitch range until rising endings and a croaky or breathy voice
   are measured (N145).
3. The levels: "Say it once, and it lands", "Let your key line land",
   "Pauses that hold the room", "A voice that moves", "Make it yours". A level
   is named as the next step of excellence. Nothing on screen says the speaker
   will be fixed or that something is wrong (N132, N134).
4. "Um" and "uh" don't count for the filler-words level (N134).
5. A coach can change the level, and a coach's change always wins. The coach
   hears blind and never sees the machine's guess first (Never 2; N129,
   N134, N140). The coach's side: [the coach panel](coach-panel.md) K (N213).

### D · Stress and readiness

1. A plan's first practice and its last practice get two ratings from 1 to
   10: how stressed and how ready the speaker feels. On the first practice
   they come at the end of the questions, before the plan card; on the last
   practice, after its Take (N148, N151, N178).
2. One question at a time: the question in the thread, its scale in the
   footer in place of "Type here". The number they tapped is never shown
   back to them; how the chat answers the tap waits for the founder's words
   (§3; N178, which changes N161's orange bubble with the number).
3. The founder and the coach see the number; Will speaks of it only in words
   (Never 1; N149, N178).
4. Every practice in between gets only "How do you feel?" (Nervous, Excited,
   Calm, Not sure), today's screen, with a Skip. Today it shows once per new
   project, before Take 1, with a Skip too (N151, N178, N211; the recording
   screens' page). With a plan it is asked only after the plan's own yes (5);
   without one it stays as today (N215), and answering is the yes for that one
   answer. The screen says "Your coach sees your answer." (N211, N217).
5. **The plan's own yes.** The personal questions (the worry question, the
   two ratings in 1, "How did it go?" at the plan's end (I2) and the feelings
   check in 4) are asked only after the speaker says yes
   to a question of its own, once. Will asks it after the speaker's voice
   answer about the event, before any personal question: "A few of my
   questions are personal: how stressed and how ready you feel, what worries
   you, and how you feel before each Take. I only ask with your yes. Your
   coach and I use your answers to shape your plan. You can turn this off in
   Settings." (N248) with Read more, which opens Privacy section 4e in a
   window, and in the footer "Not now" and "Yes, ask me"; nothing else is
   taken while Will waits. "Yes, ask me" goes on with the worry question and
   the ratings. "Not now" adds no line from Will: the worry question and the
   ratings are not asked or kept, the plan is built from what it has and
   goes on to the calls, and its end goes to "What's next?" without "How did
   it go?". A plan made before the yes existed is asked at its next personal
   moment (the last ratings, or its end). Settings has a card,
   "Personal answers in your plan", between Help improve WillpowerLab and
   WhatsApp, where the yes is turned on later or off: off asks first, then
   deletes the personal answers (the answers and their recordings, their
   bubbles in the Lounge, the plan's worry, Will's memory of them with his
   note, the feelings before a Take), and only then keeps the withdrawal; a
   deletion that fails leaves the yes on and says so. The record is the
   backend's `plan_consent_events`: the sentence's hash and the policy
   version, append-only. A record that cannot be read asks nothing personal.
   Switched off (`PLAN_CONSENT_ENABLED`) until Privacy 3.9, which names it,
   is published (N211).

### E · The plan card

1. After the calls (F), Will says "You're all set. Here's your plan." Then
   the card: under "Your next level", the level, the key line in the
   speaker's own words, their recording of it ("How you said it", from a
   small grey play button) and "Why this line?", which opens one sentence
   from what was heard. No "Ready by {date}" heading: the deadline is the
   orange line in the list (N153, N154, N157, N161, N167).
2. Under "Plan", in bullet points: the practice days ("15 minutes, 3 times
   a week: Mon, Wed, Fri", or "15 minutes every day"); in a pace or pause
   week, "This week your walk has a pace game"; "Kickoff call with your
   coach", with its time once booked; "Your deadline: {date}", the date in
   orange (for example "Sat 14 Nov"); "Summary call with your coach", with
   its time once booked. The booked times are green. A call not booked yet
   reads "Book", a link that opens the same calendar in the footer (F); then
   "Booked: {when}." Then "Change your plan" (N157, N163, N164, N167).
3. The progress bar shows on the card on a practice day and on the
   walk's end sheet, one part per Take. It fills with what the speaker did,
   never with a measure of the voice. The end sheet is a locked screen; this
   change is approved (N129, N131, N140).
4. The card uses one typeface, the app's own, and three sizes: the date, the
   text, the small labels (N161). Text colours read on white (4.5:1): the
   orange #C2410C, the green #1F7A4A, the grey #737373. Every link has a
   fingertip's tap area; the text keeps its size (N167).
5. Under the card, in place of "Type here", only "Start your first
   recording" (N161).

### F · The two calls with the coach, before the plan

1. After the ratings, before the plan card, Will says "Before I show you
   your plan, pick a time for a 15-minute call with your coach. It's your
   kickoff call." The footer, in place of "Type here", shows a calendar like
   the deadline's, with the deadline marked, then a time, then "Book". The
   kickoff comes before the deadline (N157, N161).
2. Then Will offers the summary call: "Would you also like to book your
   summary call? It's after your deadline, to look back and plan what's
   next." The calendar again, with "Skip" or "Book now"; no yes-or-no
   question first. The footer counts "1 of 2 booked" (N157, N161).
3. Either call can wait ("Skip"), so no coach stands in the way of the first
   Take (Never 3). A kickoff not booked is offered again after Take 1 (N157).
4. Calls are paid as the booking page is paid today, and free with a link
   that has no price; the button says "Book", not "Book and pay" (N143,
   N157, N161).
5. The plan's calls are these two; there is no weekly call (N157).
6. Then "You're all set. Here's your plan." and the card (E) (N161).
7. Under the calendar, "Your deadline: {date}" (for example "Sat 14 Nov")
   with a small "Edit" next to it. "Edit" turns the same calendar into the
   deadline's: "Your deadline" above it, the deadline ringed in orange, any
   day after today; with a call booked, the deadline stays after the kickoff
   and before the summary call. "Back" returns to the call; "Save" keeps the
   new date, and Will says "Done. Your plan now runs to {date}." (and "Your
   deadline is close, so you practise every day." when it is two weeks away
   or less), as "Change your plan" does. The plan follows, the calendar goes
   back to the call, and a call picked but not booked that no longer fits is
   cleared. It works before the plan and from a call's "Book" in the list
   (N168).

### G · Practice days

1. The deadline sets the journey. With two weeks or less to go, every day;
   otherwise three practices a week on days the speaker picks; with no date,
   eight weeks (N135, N140).
2. One practice is one Take with its walk, about 15 minutes (N131).
3. The chat reads in time order. A day line, small and grey, comes before
   the first message of each day, in the speaker's time zone: "Monday 26
   October" (N169).
4. Will's practice-day hello is written once per practice day: the first
   time the app opens that day, or when that day's reminder goes once there
   is one, whichever is first. Never right after a Take, never on a day off,
   not when a Take was already recorded that day. Missed practice days leave
   no trace: no catch-up hello, no "you missed"; "practice {n} of {total}"
   counts the Takes done plus one. A Take on a day off is a practice (N131,
   N169).
5. On a practice day, under the day line: "Hi Artur, good to see you back!
   Today is practice 2 of 9: Let your key line land." (N140), the bar under
   it, and "See your plan", which opens the plan in a sheet ("Your plan",
   "This week's level", the list with "Book", "Change your plan"). The plan
   card itself is posted in the chat only when the plan is made or changed.
   Progress has one unit: there is no "Week {n} of {weeks}", and "{n} of
   {total} practices done. Next: {day}." shows only after the Take, on the
   walk's end sheet (N167, N169).
6. Then today's one thing, one grey bubble drawn as the app's closing message
   is today (N89): "Today, one thing: say your key line a little slower than
   the rest. You've got this 💪" (the wedding toast: "take a breath before
   your key line"; a speaker's own case: "give your key line a little more
   room"), the key line in the speaker's words, their recording ("How you
   said it") and "● Record Take N" inside. The chat has one record button, in
   the newest bubble: the previous Take's closing message keeps its words and
   gives its button up, and a line written after it moves the bubble below.
   Once the Take is recorded the bubble gives its button up too, while the
   Take processes and its walk runs; the Take's own closing message brings
   "Record Take N" back after the walk's summary (N244).
   The last practice and the next plan show the same bubble with N89's
   closing line. No "Start a new project" under it: the "+" next to "Type
   here" opens the projects, as today (N167, N169).
7. The walk shows the Take's two best moments and the moment where this
   week's level shows most: a third moment, or one of the two when the level
   shows most there; when no moment shows it, the two best (N246 item 3, in
   place of N134 and N209's best moment and the level's: "Two best +
   level"). The level's moment opens with "This week's level: {level}.
   Here's where it shows:" above the speaker's clip, and is practised with
   the level's own practice, its row of the treatment picker: the AI
   version of the voice with its pace tip ("Let your key line land"), its
   pause tip ("Pauses that hold the room") or its stress tip ("A voice that
   moves"); the Clearer version ("Say it once, and it lands"). "Make it
   yours" keeps the walk as today. Where a level shows is read on each clip
   against the speaker's own usual on that Take, the key line's clip first
   when it shows it; never shown as a number. A walk reads the level the
   plan had when its Take was recorded, so it reads the same on every
   opening (the walk's page, H1).
8. In a pace or pause week the speaker practises their pace, from Take 2
   (Take 1 has no Ideal Text yet). The pace practice is the tempo step of
   the Feedback walk and nowhere else: not the coach panel, not the Lounge,
   not before the Take. Its rules are on the walk's page, §2 "Tempo"
   (N130, N134, N140, N142, N162). A speaker without a plan gets the same step from Take 2 when the Take has a stretch outside the green; it is the founder's "Sniper" (N181).
9. A practise that slowed down gets its own lines, said in turn: "You gave it
   room, and it landed." · "Slower, and every word counted." · "That pace
   lets them keep up with you." (N140).
10. A Take reaches its level when the check that picked the level no
   longer fires on that Take, read on the whole Take (the key line no
   longer faster than the rest; pauses no longer few and short; filler
   words no longer many; the pitch no longer flat); never shown (N209). Two
   Takes in a row that reach it, or its time running out (a week in a plan
   of up to six weeks, two weeks in a longer one; a plan of two weeks or
   less keeps one level), and the next level is the next thing heard:
   strongest first, the next level whose check fires on the latest Take,
   never one the plan already had; when none fires, "Make it yours", which
   stays. The speaker just sees it: the next hello and the plan name the
   new level, and nothing is said about their voice. A coach's level is
   never moved by the machine (C5). After three Takes on a level without
   moving, the next intervention in its row: the coach sees it in their
   queue and answers with their video, which reaches the speaker as the
   coach's word on their latest Take ([the coach panel](coach-panel.md)
   K5); the walk keeps the level's own practice (N134, N209, N213).
11. There is no practice-day reminder for now (N155). When it comes: it
   says the same words as its message in the chat, written at the same
   moment, and tapping it opens the chat at that day line. It goes at 9:00
   in the speaker's time zone by default, changed in "Change your plan", with
   no new question before Take 1. The speaker picks one of two: an email
   or a WhatsApp, the WhatsApp with the words of N154, asked as today's
   WhatsApp ask, once a privacy version names it and Meta approves its
   template; the email with its own unsubscribe link. No calendar and no
   web push. The two-option offer and the email's words wait for the
   founder's OK (N153, N154, N169, N174, N177).
12. Every reminder, on any channel: opt-in and off by default; at most one
   practice reminder a day, never twice on the same local day, between 8:00
   and 21:00 in the speaker's time zone; it says what is ready, never "come
   back", never a count of missed days, never a number about the voice
   (Never 1); outside the app the level's name at most, never the key line,
   the transcript or the recording; it stops when the plan ends; it never
   stands in the way of a Take (Never 3) (N169).
13. The proverbial 15 minutes (N180, N183): as soon as a practice day's
   practice is done, Will says in the chat "That's your 15 minutes for
   today." and "Now go and live it, away from the phone." Never on a clock
   and never on other days; nothing is locked and the message box stays.
   The founder first, then everyone.

### H · Changing the plan

1. "Change your plan" opens the plan: the deadline and the practice days are
   the speaker's to change, and the plan recalculates. The level is the
   coach's: "Ask your coach", which reaches the coach's queue as "Asked about
   their level" ([the coach panel](coach-panel.md) K1, N213). "I have a new
   event" starts the questions again (N140). While a Take is still
   processing, "I have a new event" is not shown; it comes back when the Take
   has landed, as "Record Take N" does (N242).
2. A new project asks "Is this for 14 November?" (the plan's deadline),
   after its first Take, not before it (N178). Yes keeps the plan; no starts
   "When is your deadline?" and the questions for a new plan. New slides or a
   rewrite for the same event keep the plan (N149).

### I · The plan's end

1. At the last practice, the two ratings come after the Take (D1; N178).
2. The first time the speaker opens the app on or after the deadline: "How
   did it go?" as a voice message. Then the summary call: shown with its time
   if it was booked, offered if not. Then "What's next?": "I have a new
   event" (the questions again, a new plan) or "Keep practising" (a new
   two-month plan from their Takes, no questions, its first ratings after
   its first Take) (N149, N151, N157, N178). While a Take is still
   processing, only "Keep practising" is shown (H1, N242).
3. On the deadline's morning, a short good-luck line in the chat and on the
   chosen channel; the morning after, "How did it go?" with the link outside
   the app, beside the message in the chat (2). Their words wait for the
   founder's OK (N169).

  `src/components/account/PlanConsentCard.tsx` (the card, guarded by the
  consent screens' page), `src/services/api/planConsent.ts` (backend
  `services/plan_consent.py`).
- The coach's side is the coach panel's (its page, K): `CoachPlanFlow.tsx`,
  `src/services/api/coachPlans.ts` (backend `services/plan_coach.py`).

It touches these places, each with its own page for what it owns:

- The Lounge (`Lounge.tsx`): one hook call, the plan's rows drawn from the ask
  bubbles, its footer in place of the message box, and the empty Lounge's
  button held while the plan asks. The walk's page owns the Lounge's other
  rules.
- `WillabSurface.tsx` and `src/lib/willab/setupDraft.ts`: "Start your first
  recording" opens the set-up with the topic filled in, without the feelings
  check (E5, B7).
- The recording screens (`src/components/willab/RecordingSetup.tsx`,
  `FeelingsCheckIn.tsx`): the topic filled in, and the feelings check only
  between a plan's first and last practice. Their page owns the rest.
- The consent screen (`Phase1AcceptanceGate.tsx`): "Agree and continue" opens
  the questions. Its page owns the rest.
- The walk: the level's moment, the tempo step and the bar on the end sheet.
  The walk's page owns them.
- The booking page (`src/app/book/`): the two calls are booked and paid as
  there.
